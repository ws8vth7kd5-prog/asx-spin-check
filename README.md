Upload an ASX company's results announcement to see how upbeat the language is (FinBERT tone),
the headline revenue and profit changes, and whether the tone is more positive than the numbers
justify ("spin"). Scores are ranked against 384 ASX 50 results releases (2022-2026).

Analysis only - not financial advice. In our research, neither tone nor spin predicted the share
price reaction to a release.
